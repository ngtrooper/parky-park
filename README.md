# Parky Park
A tiny physics parking puzzle: your little white wedge car has thrusters. Hop, hover and flip into impossible spots.

Play: https://ngtrooper.github.io/parky-park/

- ◀ ▶ drive / air-steer · ▲ / SPACE thrusters · Q / E spin · R instant retry · Enter next · Esc levels
- `npm run dev` to play locally, `node tools/solve.mjs` proves every level is 3-star beatable in the real sim,
  `node tools/browser_test.mjs` replays those solutions in headless Chromium.

Parody game. Not affiliated with any car or rocket company.
