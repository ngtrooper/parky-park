Spot Thief: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · Publisher: Spot Thief developer · Contact: ng.trooper@gmail.com

Spot Thief is a casual game. This policy explains what information the game and its ad partner handle. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. No accounts, no personal data collected by us

Spot Thief has no sign-up, login, chat or user-generated content. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

2. Game progress is stored on your device

Your coins, upgrades, unlocked paints, settings and best scores are saved locally on your device (app storage / browser storage). This data never leaves your device unless your platform backs it up (for example Android device backup, or a web game portal's own cloud save if you play there while logged in to that portal). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. You can also erase it in-game with Shop → Reset progress.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) in the mobile app

The Android and iOS apps show ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded video ads (for example "Watch ad: 2x coins") and occasional full-screen ads between runs, never during gameplay. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA where permitted), IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

  • Google's practices are described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. On iOS, the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

When you play Spot Thief on a web game portal (for example CrazyGames, Poki or GameDistribution), ads and any account features there are provided by that portal under its own privacy policy.

4. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We receive only aggregated ad revenue reports from our ad partners.

5. Children

Spot Thief is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Security and retention

Because game progress stays on your device, it is retained until you delete it. Information collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of personal data. Since we hold no personal data on our side, most requests are handled through your device settings and Google's tools linked above; you can also contact us at ng.trooper@gmail.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if the game adds features. The latest version is always available at this page, with the effective date above.
