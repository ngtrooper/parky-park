FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 max):
The lot is full. Every car can fly. Only one gets the spot.

Spot Thief is a physics stunt-parking game. Boost down a giant ramp, launch into the sky and pilot your car with real thrusters (lift, push forward, brake and slam down) through a true momentum arc. Flip in mid-air for bonus pay, then land and stop in a free parking spot before a rival driver steals it.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drive with the left-thumb arrows.
• Push the right-thumb THRUST pad to fire the car's jets. Push it forward on the ramp to boost.
• Flip with the rotate buttons. Each clean flip adds +25% to your pay, but only if you park!
• Watch the landing marker: GREEN means a free spot. Land, stop, and it's yours.

FEATURES
• Real-momentum flying car physics with an 8-way thruster pad
• Rival drivers and snatchers racing you to the same spot
• Farther spots pay more: build your perfect launch
• 7 upgrade lines with 60+ levels: Boost Power, Air Tank, Thrusters, Boost Tank, Tires, Spin Gyro, Coin Magnet
• Paint shop with 13 paints, including animated Chrome, Neon and Holo
• 8 thruster flame colours, from Ion Blue to Rainbow
• Daily Lot challenge and a daily coin bonus
• Hand-made puzzle levels
• Play offline. Progress saves on your device.
• Optional rewarded ads for 2x coins. No ads during gameplay.

Can you steal spot #10?
